How to read this site

Three depths, same subject

Astronomy is usually written either for children or for physicists. CosmoVerse writes every subject at three depths and lets you choose which one you want today.

Depth 01

Explore

Plain language, visual first, no assumed background. Answers the question you would actually ask out loud: what is a black hole?

Depth 02

Learn

The mechanism, with the numbers kept in. How a star above roughly twenty solar masses ends up collapsing past its own escape velocity.

Depth 03

Discover

Individual objects, structured and cited. Sagittarius A*: 4.3 million solar masses, 26,000 light years away, imaged in 2022.

About

Accurate first, beautiful anyway

CosmoVerse is an educational project. Every figure comes from published observation, every diagram states what it compresses, and nothing is exaggerated to look impressive — the real numbers already are.

The whole site is hand-built HTML and CSS. No frameworks, no libraries, no JavaScript. Every planet, star, ring and spiral you see is drawn with gradients and geometry.
